# ISMACONNECT Soft-Launch Seed Pack

This seed pack is for **real local inventory**, not fake activity. The goal is to make the first Fort McMurray launch feel useful in the categories where ISMACONNECT is strongest.

## 1. Priority categories

Seed these first, in this order:

1. `Ride Share`
2. `Services`
3. `Rentals`
4. `Jobs`

Keep `Buy & Sell` active, but do not spend most of the first-week seeding effort there.

## 2. Minimum launch targets

- `Ride Share`: 12 to 15 live listings
- `Services`: 15 to 20 live listings
- `Rentals`: 8 to 12 live listings
- `Jobs`: 8 to 10 live listings
- `Buy & Sell`: 6 to 8 live listings

## 3. Address entry rule

Use these rules consistently:

- If the poster wants privacy:
  - `Thickwood, Fort McMurray`
  - `Downtown, Fort McMurray`
  - `Timberlea, Fort McMurray`
- If the poster wants an exact business map pin:
  - full street address, for example `205 Powder Drive, Fort McMurray`
- Camp-based jobs or transport:
  - `Site / camp`
- If the user is unsure:
  - `Fort McMurray`

## 4. Real listing templates to source

### Ride Share

- Daily Timberlea to site gate morning ride
- Thickwood to airport early pickup
- Fort McMurray to Edmonton weekend trip
- Camp shuttle seats available
- Tool-friendly work commute ride

### Services

- Move-out cleaning
- Dump run / junk removal
- Snow removal or yard cleanup
- Handyman / small repair visits
- Carpet cleaning
- Moving help

### Rentals

- Private room in Thickwood
- Basement suite in Timberlea
- Furnished short-term rental for workers
- Apartment near downtown
- Parking or storage listing

### Jobs

- Camp kitchen helper
- Class 1 driver
- Site cleaner
- Weekend service worker
- Skilled trades helper

## 5. First-week sourcing plan

- Ask 5 trusted local contacts to post one listing each
- Manually onboard 5 service providers
- Manually onboard 3 ride-share posters
- Add 3 to 5 rental posters
- Add 3 to 5 local job posters

## 6. Trust rules

Do not seed:

- fake reviews
- fake verification
- fake businesses
- fake exact addresses

It is better to have fewer real posts than a fuller marketplace that feels manufactured.

## 7. What to watch after seeding

- response rate to the seeded listings
- repeat posting from the same sellers
- saved searches created in rides, services, rentals, and jobs
- which community names appear most often on the map

## 8. Decision rule

If one category gets replies and repeat posting faster than the others, make that category even more visible on the homepage and in local outreach.
