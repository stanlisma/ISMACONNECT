# ISMACONNECT Business Outreach Playbook

Ready-to-use scripts and workflow for Track C manual outreach, built 2026-07-30.
Source: Copilot chat notes (`ISMACONNECT Launch.pdf`), condensed and reordered around what's
actually live on the site today (claim flow + Founding Member Program).

## The goal

**100 real Services listings before worrying about anything else.** Not traffic, not ads,
not a referral program — supply. A marketplace with a handful of listings feels dead no
matter how many visitors show up; a marketplace with 100 active local listings starts
selling itself.

Focus almost entirely on **Services** for now (cleaners, painters, movers, detailers,
tutors, caterers, photographers, landscapers, handypeople) — easiest category to onboard,
and once it looks active it pulls Rentals/Jobs/Ride Share along naturally. Don't chase all
four categories at once.

**Pace:** aim for 4–10 businesses onboarded per outreach session. At 4/day that's 100 in 25
days; a couple of focused "bulk onboarding" afternoons (2 hours, walking a commercial strip)
can do 10+ in one sitting.

## How onboarding actually works on the site (do this, not sign-up links)

Do **not** ask a business to create an account. Most won't. Instead:

1. Get their info in under 2 minutes (see checklist below).
2. Go to **[/admin/storefronts](https://ismaconnect.ca/admin/storefronts)** → create their business as an **unclaimed storefront**. This
   auto-tags it as a **Founding Member** if a spot is still open (currently under the 100 cap).
3. Post their first listing yourself from your own account via **Post a listing**, selecting
   their storefront in the "attach to storefront" field so it's tied to the business, not to you personally.
4. That's it — their business is live, publicly visible, with a "Founding Member" badge and an
   "Unclaimed" badge on their storefront page.
5. Whenever the real owner is ready, send them their storefront link. They sign up (or sign in),
   click **Claim this business**, and once you approve it from **[/admin/claims](https://ismaconnect.ca/admin/claims)**, the
   storefront *and* every listing under it transfer to their account automatically — no data
   re-entry, nothing lost. They keep the Founding Member badge; it survives the claim.

This is genuinely useful to mention to skeptical business owners: **you're not locked into
us managing your page — claim it any time, for free, and it's fully yours.**

## What to collect per business (2-minute intake)

- Business name
- Phone number
- Website or Facebook page URL
- Logo (photo is fine, or ask them to text/email one)
- Service description (1–3 sentences — you can also just paraphrase what they tell you)
- Service area(s) / neighbourhoods served
- Hours (if relevant)

## The core pitch (memorize this, adapt naturally)

> "Hi, I'm [name], I run ISMA Connect — a local Fort McMurray marketplace where residents
> find rides, rentals, jobs, and local services. I'm featuring local businesses for free
> right now. I'd love to add yours — it takes about 2 minutes, no cost, no obligation. Can I
> grab your logo and contact info?"

Keep it simple. No jargon, no sales pressure — you're doing them a favor, not asking one.

If they ask "what's the catch": *"No catch — free listing, and if you ever want your own
login to manage it directly, I'll send you a claim link any time."*

## Method 1 — Walk-in visits (best channel for Fort McMurray)

Best targets: barbers, restaurants, cleaning companies, auto shops, movers, home service
companies. Walk in, say the core pitch above in person, collect the checklist info on the
spot, create the storefront + listing that evening.

**Bulk onboarding days:** pick a commercial strip (Thickwood, Timberlea, Downtown), block 2
hours, aim for 10 stops. Even a 50% acceptance rate is 5 new businesses in an afternoon.

## Method 2 — Facebook group outreach

Search Fort McMurray Facebook groups for terms like "Fort McMurray Cleaning," "Fort
McMurray Movers," "Fort McMurray Handyman," "Fort McMurray Catering," then DM the business
page or poster directly:

> "Hi! I run ISMA Connect, a local Fort McMurray marketplace. I'd like to feature your
> business on our platform at no charge. If you're interested, send me your business name,
> services, and contact info and I'll create the listing for you."

No sales pitch, no pressure — you're doing the work, they're just handing over info.

## Method 3 — Facebook group demand-mining

Fort McMurray community groups constantly have posts like *"Looking for a ride to CNRL
tomorrow,"* *"Basement suite for rent,"* *"Need house cleaning,"* *"Anyone know a
handyman?"* — every one is a live signal of unmet demand. Reply with value, not spam:

> "We've got a dedicated Fort McMurray board for rides and services — feel free to post it
> there too, might get you more replies."

This also surfaces businesses to onboard directly (the handyman someone recommends in the
replies is your next outreach target).

## Method 4 — Personal network

You already know operators, tradespeople, electricians, mechanics, welders, instrument
techs, contractors, supervisors — many run side businesses. Message them directly:

> "I remember you were doing [pressure washing / side work] — can I list your business on
> ISMA Connect? Free, takes 2 minutes, I'll set it up for you."

Personal introductions convert far better than cold outreach. Even a 1-in-5 hit rate across
your network can produce dozens of listings.

## Method 5 — Lead with the Founding Member Program

This is what makes the pitch land better than "can I put you on my website?" — and it's now
a real, live feature (not just marketing copy):

- Free listing, forever
- Founding Member badge on their storefront page
- Featured placement in the "Founding Business Member Program" banner on **[/businesses](https://ismaconnect.ca/businesses)**
  while spots remain (currently well under the 100 cap)
- Early access to new features as the platform grows

Frame it as exclusivity and recognition for joining early — not a discount. People like
being one of the first in on something new.

## Weekly targets

| Week | Cumulative Services listings | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20–30 | Mostly walk-ins + personal network — fastest close rate |
| 2 | 45–60 | Add Facebook outreach + group demand-mining |
| 3–4 | 80–100+ | Bulk onboarding days to close the gap |

Once Services is past ~100 listings and looking genuinely active, expand outreach to
Rentals, Ride Share, and Jobs using the same playbook — and revisit the referral program
(Track A4) once there's a real user base to make "invite 3 businesses, get a featured
listing" meaningful.
